Institutional 4-Level Approval Process

Sequential, tamper-proof verification designed for complete transparency and accountability.

LEVEL 1

Scholarship Officer

Verifies student identity, income certificates, and basic scheme eligibility criteria.

LEVEL 2

Department HOD

Validates academic performance, attendance records, discipline, and course progress.

LEVEL 3

College Principal

Performs institutional executive review and grants final sanction for scholarship awards.

LEVEL 4

Fund Disbursement

Financial treasury DBT transfer to student bank account with verifiable audit logging.
